COMMON QUESTION

How often should I visit Lumina Dental Care for a checkup and cleaning?

We recommend scheduling a routine checkup and professional cleaning every six months to maintain optimal oral health. Regular visits allow our team to detect early signs of decay and keep your smile radiant before minor issues become major concerns.

Does Lumina Dental Care offer options for nervous or anxious patients

Yes, patient comfort is our top priority, and we strive to make every visit as stress-free as possible. We offer a calm environment, gentle care, and mild sedation options like nitrous oxide to keep you completely relaxed throughout your appointment.

What is the difference between in-office whitening and at-home kits

In-office whitening uses specialized light technology to deliver immediate, dramatic results in just a single 60-minute visit. Custom take-home kits offer a flexible, gradual alternative using professional-grade gels that allow you to brighten your smile at your own pace.

What should I do if I experience a sudden dental emergency

If you experience a severe toothache, a chipped tooth, or a lost restoration, contact Lumina Dental Care immediately for prompt assistance. We reserve daily priority slots to ensure emergency patients receive fast, compassionate relief when they need it most.
